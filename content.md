# About

Hello! I am Shuang Chen. I currently work for the AI inference infrastructure team at Bytedance Seed. Prior to this, I focused on improving resource efficiency for AI inference infrastructure (mainly on Ascend NPUs) and public cloud platforms in Huawei. 

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Cornell University in 2021, under the supervision of [Prof. José F. Martínez](https://martinez.csl.cornell.edu) and [Prof. Christina Delimitrou](https://people.csail.mit.edu/delimitrou/). My [thesis](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/bitstream/handle/1813/109725/Chen_cornellgrad_0058F_12468.pdf?sequence=1) is on QoS-aware resource management of interactive services in datacenters. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from the ACM Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2015.

I am broadly interested in computer architecture and computer systems. Starting from 2023, my engineering and research efforts have centered on developing efficient LLM infrastructure, especially on non-GPU AI platforms. For instance, supporting LLM multi-tenancy on Huawei Ascend NPUs, designing asynchronous inference systems on AI supernodes, and developing ultra-low-latency inference systems on customized AI chips.
I am dedicated to fully unlocking the potential of AI chips and hardware architectures, continuously pushing the frontier of AI performance and cost efficiency.
